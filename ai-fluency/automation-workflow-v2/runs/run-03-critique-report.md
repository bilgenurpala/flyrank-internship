## Coverage gaps

- **Structured vs. unstructured content (§4).** Named in the draft, then dropped. **Should be added** — it is the only Pack content bearing on *what a gate can display back*, and the draft's own framing ("bear on what a gate can show") sets up a point it never makes.
- **Protocol errors vs. tool execution errors (§4).** Named, unused. **Should be considered** — the draft flags "what happens on denial" as a silence, and this distinction is the nearest Pack material to that question. The Pack does not place denial in either error class, which is itself the finding.
- **"Effectively deny requests" (§1).** The qualifier "effectively" is never examined. **Should be added** — the research question asks what makes a gate *meaningful*, and this is the Pack's own hedge on whether denial capability suffices. The Pack does not unpack it.
- **The "Tools" definition (§2) — interaction with external systems, APIs, databases.** Quoted in the concepts table, never used in reasoning. *Optional.* It is the Pack's only statement about what tools reach, which is the implicit stake in exfiltration.
- **Confidence ratings (§3).** Surfaced once, at the end. *Optional* — adequately handled.
- **§7 source-coverage structure.** Used, but misused (C14). Should be retained in corrected form rather than removed.

No gap here leaves the research question unanswered. The draft's defects are C1–C3, which concern precision about what the Pack claims, not omission.

## Critique limitations

This audit compares the Draft Note to the Evidence Pack. It cannot establish:

- **Whether the Pack is faithful to its sources.** Every finding treats the Pack as ground truth. Loose paraphrase, dropped qualifiers, or misassignment inside the Pack passes through this critique unflagged — including on the seventeen rows marked acceptable.
- **Whether any "source mismatch" finding is possible in principle.** All source-reference fields in §3 are blank. C5 is marked source mismatch only against the Pack's *internal* characterisation, not against a document. No finding in this table can be traced to an original source.
- **Whether the C2 conflict is real or an artefact of merging.** The "always deny-capable" passage and the "sensitive operations" framing may come from one source or from two, possibly at different protocol versions. With blank references, I cannot tell whether the Pack recorded a genuine tension in the material or created one during synthesis. This is the largest unresolved risk in the chain and it determines whether C2's required change is "surface a conflict" or "surface an assembly error."
- **Whether the quoted passages are verbatim or where they begin and end.** The stray comma artefacts mark stripped citation positions; they do not mark quotation boundaries.
- **Whether SHOULD and MUST appear in the originals with RFC 2119 force.** The entire C3/C4 line of findings depends on this and rests on the Pack's inference alone.
- **Whether the "High" confidence ratings are warranted.** They are the Pack's self-assessment with no stated basis.
- **Whether the Pack omitted source material relevant to the research question.** Coverage gaps above are gaps between draft and Pack, not between draft and sources.
